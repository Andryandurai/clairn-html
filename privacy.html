Clairn privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version. Clairn keeps your work on your own device. The developer runs no server for the app and receives none of your project data. Sync, GitHub and the connectors work only if you switch them on, and they talk to services that you choose and own, not to the developer. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What this covers

The Clairn app (Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android and the browser preview), the Clairn collector that runs on your computer, and this website. The Clairn for Chrome extension has its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your projects, notes, activity, focus sessions, checkpoints, settings and any background picture are stored in a local database on your device. Access tokens (the collector pairing token, a GitHub token, your sync login) are kept in the system's secure storage where the platform has one. In the browser preview they last only for that page session.

Device sync and who can see what

Sync uses a Supabase project that someone set up: usually you, or a person who invited you. Everything is encrypted on your device with a recovery key that never leaves your devices, before it is uploaded. The owner of that Supabase project, and Supabase as the host, can see your account email, a vault ID, device IDs, sizes and times, and the encrypted data. They cannot read what is inside it. If you use a project run by someone else, that person owns it and can see those technical details and your email. The developer of Clairn sees none of this unless the developer is also the owner of the project you use.

The website

The early-list form sends the email address you type to the Clairn developer through FormSubmit, a third-party service that forwards it by email, and it may be added to a spreadsheet. It is used only to tell you about Clairn, and kept until you ask for it to be removed or the early list ends. The page sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs under GitHub's own policy. The Instagram link opens Instagram, under Instagram's policy.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • On your device: Settings can export your records and delete all local data.
  • Sync: Disconnect this device stops syncing here and keeps your records. Delete my account (Settings, Continue on another device) deletes your account, your encrypted vault, your synced pictures and the stored connection details for Linear, Jira and Google Calendar from the Supabase project. Clairn first asks each provider to revoke its access; for Jira you should also remove Clairn in your Atlassian account settings. Records on your devices are kept. If someone else owns the project you use, they hold the project itself.
  • Connectors: Disconnect asks the provider to revoke access.
  • Website: email the address below and your sign-up is removed.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete personal data, and to complain to a regulator. Write to the address below and the developer will help with anything the developer holds.

Security

Sync data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload, and travels over HTTPS. Provider credentials are encrypted before they are stored. The collector listens only on your own computer and needs a pairing token. No system is perfectly secure, and you should keep your recovery key somewhere safe: nobody, including the developer, can recover your data without it.

Children

Clairn is not directed at children. If you believe a child has signed up for the early list, write to the address below.
